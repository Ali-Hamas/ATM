DemoBank Cardless ATM — User Guide

A 5-minute walkthrough: start the server, open the ATM screen, scan the QR with your phone, and withdraw cash. No physical card required.

What you need

ItemWhy
Go 1.22+ installedTo run the backend (one binary serves everything)
Node.js 20+ and npmOnly to run the mobile app via Expo
A phone with Expo GoThe phone is your "bank app" — it scans the ATM's QR
Phone and laptop on the same Wi-FiSo the phone can reach your laptop at port 4000

Step-by-step

1

Start the backend (this also serves the ATM screen)

Open a terminal and run:

cd "M:\Banking app\backend-go"
go run ./cmd/server

You'll see auth middleware listening on :4000. Leave this running.

2

Open the ATM in your browser

Go to http://localhost:4000. You'll see the DemoBank ATM kiosk screen with the gold side-buttons and live clock.

Click Continue → Cardless Cash Withdrawal. A QR code appears inside a gold scanning ring with a 60-second countdown.

3

Start the mobile app (your "bank app")

In a second terminal:

cd "M:\Banking app\mobile-app"
npm install
npx expo start

On your phone, open Expo Go and scan the QR that Expo shows in your terminal. The bank app loads on your phone.

If the phone can't connect, edit API_BASE in mobile-app/App.tsx to your laptop's Wi-Fi IPv4 (find it with ipconfig). Allow port 4000 through Windows Firewall when prompted.

4

Scan the ATM's QR with your phone

In the bank app on your phone:

  • Tap Cardless ATM Access
  • Grant camera permission the first time
  • Point the camera at the QR shown on the ATM screen (the one in your browser)

The app reads the QR's JWT, sends it to the backend, and shows an Approve with biometrics (mocked) button.

5

Approve and withdraw

Tap Approve. Within ~1 second the ATM browser tab flips to Authenticated — that's the WebSocket push from the backend.

Pick an amount (£20 / £50 / £100 / £200). The ATM plays the cash-dispense animation, prints a receipt strip, and resets to idle after a few seconds.

What success looks like

StageYou should see
ATM idle"DEMOBANK" wordmark, "Please insert your card or use cardless access"
QR shownGold scanning ring, circular countdown timer, animated scan-line
Phone approvedATM flips to "Welcome, <name>" with green checkmark
After amount picked"Please take your cash" with downward dispense animation

Quick test without a phone

To prove the backend works end-to-end without the mobile app, run the included PowerShell script:

cd "M:\Banking app"
.\test-demo.ps1

It creates a session, approves it as "Ali Hassan", and prints the audit log. Watch your ATM browser tab — it flips to Authenticated as soon as the script approves.

Troubleshooting

SymptomFix
App stuck on "Authorising…"Phone can't reach laptop. Check API_BASE and firewall on port 4000.
QR shows "Session expired"60s TTL elapsed. Click Start over, or set SESSION_TTL_SECONDS=120 before starting backend.
go: command not foundInstall Go from go.dev/dl, then re-open your terminal.
Browser shows old UI after editingRebuild the UI: cd atm-simulator && npm run build, copy dist/* into backend-go/internal/web/static/, restart Go server.
